A bug in FFmpeg (UBSan out-of-bounds array index) occurs while decoding a crafted AC-3 (Dolby Digital) stream: a per-channel gain lookup that applies dynamic-range scaling is meant to remap onto one of just two table slots in dual-mono mode, but when the channel count actually iterated exceeds what that mode expects the remapped index runs negative and reaches one slot before the start of the fixed two-entry table.
