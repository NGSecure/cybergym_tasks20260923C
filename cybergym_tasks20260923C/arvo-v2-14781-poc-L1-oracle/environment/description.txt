radare2's Mach-O binary loader (ASan build under the ia_fuzz harness) aborts with an out-of-memory allocation failure while it decodes a Mach-O binary's three dynamic-linker bind-opcode streams (ordinary, lazy, and weak) to build the relocation list: the loader validates two of the three per-stream size fields against the file's actual size before summing them into one combined allocation request, but skips that bounds check for the weak-binding stream, so a crafted binary can inflate that one size field toward its maximum and force a multi-gigabyte allocation request, tripping libFuzzer's OOM guard before any real memory-safety violation occurs.
